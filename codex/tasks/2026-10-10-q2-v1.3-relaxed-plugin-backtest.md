## 4. Q2 v1.3 — frozen final relaxed rules

Create a new independent plugin version. Do not overwrite Q2 v1.1/v1.2.

Suggested identity:

- strategy/plugin id: `q2_relaxed_channel_v1_3`
- research method: `high-beta-liquid-channel-v1.3`
- plugin semantic version: `1.3.0`

### 4.1 Market gate — keep the original high-elasticity idea

Do **not** loosen these merely to create trades:

- SPY 126-session Beta >= **2.0**
- ADV20 >= **USD 50 million**
- preferred tier: Beta >= **2.5** and ADV20 >= **USD 100 million**

Use causal paired returns and causal historical price/volume evidence. If the Research Infrastructure source cannot support a field safely on a date, exclude and count it.

### 4.2 Channel structure — deliberately less rigid than v1.2

Keep the broad Q2 idea: repeated range/channel behavior, not monotonic collapse, and current price in the lower half.

Use weekly windows `12/16/20/26` and monthly windows `9/12/15/18` when history permits.

Relative to v1.2, pre-register these relaxations:

- minimum complete swings: **1.0** instead of 1.5;
- minimum clarity: **55** instead of 62;
- normalized 126-session drift acceptance: approximately **-15% to +25%** instead of -10% to +18%;
- preserve basic width / boundary-contact / coverage / approximate-parallel sanity checks;
- **remove adjacent-window consensus as a hard admission gate**;
- one sufficiently good eligible weekly **or** monthly window can establish the structure;
- if multiple windows are available, consistency/stability becomes a **ranking/diagnostic bonus or penalty**, not an automatic rejection unless the implementation detects an obviously contradictory/invalid fit;
- do not require both weekly and monthly timeframes to qualify.

This is intentional: v1.3 is testing whether v1.2's "textbook-perfect channel" definition was too narrow.

### 4.3 Current price location

Use log-position within the chosen channel/range:

- preferred low region: **0%–35%**;
- secondary acceptable region: **35%–55%**;
- >55% is not an entry candidate;
- clear structural breakdown remains rejected;
- mild undercut may remain a risk flag rather than automatic promotion.

### 4.4 Entry readiness — score confirmations instead of requiring all of them

Keep these four causal confirmations:

1. higher-low evidence;
2. two rising closes;
3. improving close-location / close placement;
4. close at or above 5-session mean.

v1.2 effectively demanded an overly strict conjunction. v1.3 should:

- require **at least 1 of the 4** confirmations for selection;
- use the number/strength of confirmations in the score;
- still reject a clear breakdown;
- still block obviously severe downside acceleration;
- still treat a one-day >8% rise or five-day >15% rise as extended/wait rather than chase.

Define "severe downside acceleration" causally and version it. It should be materially stricter than the old generic `falling` flag, so an ordinary pullback is not automatically disqualified.

### 4.5 Ranking

Keep ranking simple and auditable. Suggested score components:

- 50% structure/clarity;
- 25% readiness confirmations;
- 15% lower-position attractiveness;
- 10% preferred Beta/ADV tier.

Exact normalization must be documented and frozen before any portfolio return is viewed.

Select at most **Top 10** per signal date. Do not pad invalid names.
