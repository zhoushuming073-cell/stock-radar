The user confirmed a new workflow combining Quant fuzzy candidate generation, SVG charts for human inspection, two-stage labels and independent future-outcome supervision.

**The left side contains historical observations through T close only.** Before future reveal, the user saves immutable H_blind (Observe / Entry / Confidence). **The right side is revealed only after server-verified H_blind save**; T+1 to T+10 data is used for retrospective H_review and independently computed objective Y_future. The vertical separation marks the **decision information cutoff**, not a guaranteed T close fill; first hypothetical entry is T+1 tradeable open.

SVG is preferred for lossless human zoom; machine benchmarks may use normalized numeric sequences, deterministic past-only SVG rasterization, or an explicitly defined geometry encoder. Neither right-side data nor its auto-scaling may influence left-side images, numeric windows, task metadata, Quant sampling or model inputs.

Later model experiments will compare Quant-only, sequence-only, Vision-H, Vision-Y and Fusion under the same fixed evaluation contract. H_review is a retrospective explanatory label **not** a substitute for H_blind or an independent financial outcome.
