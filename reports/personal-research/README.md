# Personal Research

> This directory is reserved for the owner's personal research reports and manually written investment/strategy research conclusions.

## Scope

Put here:

- personal strategy research reports;
- manually written experiment conclusions;
- comparative research between strategies;
- research notes intended to be preserved as a report;
- personal interpretation of exported Scanner / Backtest evidence.

Do not put here:

- project journey logs or engineering backlogs;
- automated acceptance / verification logs;
- raw Research Export Bundles;
- generated datasets or large CSV/Parquet artifacts;
- Phase 2 historical engine artifacts.

## Recommended filename convention

`research-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM-topic.md`

If a report has a lifecycle status, append one of:

- `draft`
- `final`
- `superseded`

Example:

`research-2026-09-30-1510-three-strategy-comparison-final.md`

## Relationship to other report directories

- `reports/journey-*`: engineering/project status history.
- `reports/phase2/`: historical pre-v3 research-engine artifacts.
- `reports/personal-research/`: owner-authored research reports and conclusions.

Personal research reports should cite the Run IDs, Research Bundle IDs, dataset snapshot, strategy versions, or other evidence they rely on whenever available.

## Preserved historical reports

- `research-2026-09-27-scanner-validation-draft.md` — first Scanner validation research draft.
- `research-2026-09-27-project-stage-1-superseded.md` — historical project assessment; use the current ledger in `reports/README.md` for present status.
