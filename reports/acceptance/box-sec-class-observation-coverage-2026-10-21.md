For source examples, the [HLX 2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/866829/000155837025001617/hlx-20241231x10k.htm), [FFWM 2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1413837/000155837025003129/ffwm-20241231x10k.htm), and [ONTF 2024 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110611/000111061125000004/ontf-20241231.htm) disclose their respective common class, trading symbol and exchange. The script validates local review structure and chronology; it does not download, hash or automatically parse the filing bytes. A single earlier filing cannot prove no ticker, share-class, issuer or listing change between filing and candidate T.
