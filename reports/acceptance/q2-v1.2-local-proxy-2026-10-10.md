### Structural bottleneck check

A read-only, outcome-free drilldown on 2023-08-02 and 2025-02-05 covered 53 and
77 market-qualified securities respectively. Among these 130, 125 had **zero**
eligible weekly/monthly channel windows and five had **one** eligible window;
none satisfied the required multi-window consensus. Of their 1,040 individual
window fits, `alternation` failed 1,006, `quality` 972, `contacts` 968 and
normalized `drift` 813 times. These failures overlap within the same window,
so the counts must not be added. This is a two-day diagnostic sample, not an
all-date estimate of each subgate. Across all 58 signal dates, the aggregate
channel reasons were `no_clear_channel` 3,083 and
`channel_windows_disagree` 123. The daily entry-readiness step was never
reached for a fully structure-qualified candidate. The evidence points to a
restrictive **channel construction/consensus layer**, not to LEAN fills or a
profit/loss rule. Threshold changes would require separate shape review and
a new version; v1.2 was not loosened from these observations.

Both real local Native QuantConnect LEAN invocations completed and reconciled
to the standard Stock Radar result adapter. Initial capital and final equity
were USD 1,000,000 in each run, max drawdown 0, fees 0, and open positions 0.
These runs validate the empty-signal execution/presentation path only; they do
**not** validate actual entry, exit, size, cost or fill behavior for v1.2 trades.

## Source, method and limits

- Frozen Research Infrastructure v1 semantic hash:
  `74b765911a8a4f18b2074cd8d9e529b25b54afade15309a148b0ca43340b80be`.
  Candidate membership is reconstructed `confirmed_member` / `probable_member`
  in the supported 126-session universe; the selected OHLCV source is the
  homogeneous `legacy:alpaca:sip` split series. The local SPY/QQQ references
  are Alpaca SIP split series. The 430-session prefix is clipped at T; no
  future outcomes are read during candidate generation.
- Historical identity is not a certified full-market census. The legacy vendor
  adjustment does not independently certify historical absolute liquidity,
  corporate actions or disappeared-security coverage. This adapter labels the
  evidence as an **uncertified proxy**; formal historical certification was
  neither asserted nor bypassed in the ordinary selector path.
- Native LEAN build commit `82810ea63d5542f05dc58f9d1db0557cf174d0ea`;
  Top 10, up to 10 positions, next-session Open proxy, tenth-session Close,
  USD 1m, 10 bps per side, illustrative frozen fee schedule. Minute market
  data, auction prices, dividends and terminal entitlements are incomplete.
- Study configuration: [q2_v12_local_proxy.yaml](../../config/q2_v12_local_proxy.yaml).
  Runner: [run_q2_v12_local_proxy.py](../../scripts/run_q2_v12_local_proxy.py).
  The runner's post-run SHA256 is
  `6acac4e1c8616ca4bf66a7bddf92184e89408e5bc37a4cf2022ba62df91eb1e1`;
  its code was not part of the pre-scan receipt, while the selector, configs,
  frozen data semantic hash and LEAN build were. This is a reproducibility
  limitation of this first adapter.
- Private receipts, per-day funnels, signal JSONL, execution gates, native
  journals and normalized results are under
  `data/research/q2-v12-local-proxy/{train,validation}/`. These remain ignored
  local files; no market bars, identity mapping or account material is committed.
